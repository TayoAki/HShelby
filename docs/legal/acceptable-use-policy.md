# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everyone who uses Hire Shelby: Customers, their Team Members, and the apps that include our SDK. It's part of our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md). "You" means the Customer, and you're responsible for your Team Members and your apps following it.

Hire Shelby is for helping the people who use your app. Use it for honest, wanted conversations with them, and don't use it to harm them, us or anyone else.

## 1. No spam or unwanted messages

- Message only people who use your app and whose messages relate to it.
- Don't use automations, offers, cards or push notifications for unsolicited bulk messaging, or for marketing to people who haven't agreed to it where the law or Apple's rules require consent. Apple allows promotional push notifications only to people who opted in through consent language in your app and who can opt out.
- Honour opt-outs and requests to stop.
- Don't impersonate Apple, another company or another person, or disguise who a message comes from.

## 2. No illegal or harmful content

Don't use the Service to send, store or request content that:

- is illegal where you, the recipient or we are, including child sexual abuse material (which we report to the authorities);
- infringes anyone's intellectual property, privacy or publicity rights;
- contains malware, phishing or links to either; or
- promotes violence, terrorism, or self-harm.

## 3. No harassment

Don't threaten, harass, bully, stalk or intimidate anyone, publish their private information, or send hateful content targeting people for who they are.

## 4. No deceptive offers or reviews

- Offers must be accurate: describe prices, trials, discounts and terms truthfully, and only send offers that match the paywall your app will show.
- Don't invent urgency or scarcity that isn't real, or pressure vulnerable people, including minors, to buy.
- Sell digital content and features inside your app only through the purchase methods Apple's rules allow.
- Don't offer anything in return for ratings or reviews, ask only for genuine ones, and make sure review-request cards follow Apple's rules on review prompts.

## 5. No sensitive data

Don't ask for, send or store these in chats, identity fields, custom metadata, help docs or AI instructions:

- health or medical information;
- payment card numbers or bank account details;
- passwords, one-time codes or other credentials;
- government ID numbers (such as passport, driving licence or social security numbers);
- precise location; or
- other special categories of data, such as data about racial or ethnic origin, religion, sexual orientation, biometrics or criminal records.

If someone volunteers such data in a chat, don't copy it elsewhere, and delete it when you no longer need it. The Service isn't designed for regulated data such as protected health information under HIPAA or cardholder data under PCI DSS.

## 6. No children's apps without agreement

Don't use the Service in apps in Apple's Kids Category or apps directed to children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where your users live) without our prior written agreement.

## 7. No misuse of AI Replies

- Don't instruct the AI to claim to be human, to hide that it's an AI, or to impersonate a real person.
- Don't use AI Replies for legal, medical, financial or other professional advice, for safety-critical uses, or to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on people.
- Don't use AI Replies to manipulate people, to exploit vulnerabilities such as age or financial hardship, or to make promises your team won't keep (such as refunds).
- Don't put content in help docs or instructions that tries to get around the AI's rules or guardrails, or test the AI with prompt injection against other Customers or our systems.
- Follow the usage policies of our AI provider, Anthropic.

## 8. No attacks on the Service

- Don't access or try to access another Customer's workspace or data, or any part of the Service you're not authorised to use.
- Don't probe, scan or test the Service's security except under our [vulnerability disclosure policy](./security.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).
- Don't interfere with or overload the Service, including denial-of-service attacks, and don't upload malicious files.
- Don't reverse engineer, decompile or copy the Service, except as the law allows despite this restriction.
- Don't use the Service to build a competing product.

## 9. No getting around limits

- Don't circumvent rate limits, quotas (such as the image library's storage limit), feature gates or billing, for example by spreading requests over many devices or addresses or by creating extra workspaces.
- Don't share accounts or resell the Service.
- If you need higher limits, for a load test or anything else, ask us at support@hireshelby.com.

## 10. Follow third parties' rules

Follow the terms of the services you connect to Hire Shelby, including Apple's App Review Guidelines and Developer Program License Agreement, Telegram's terms, and your subscription providers' terms.

## Enforcement

If we reasonably believe this policy has been broken, we may remove content, turn off a feature for an app, suspend access or terminate the Agreement, as the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) describe, and we may report illegal activity to the authorities. Where we can, we'll tell you first and give you a chance to fix the problem.

## Reporting abuse

To report a breach of this policy, email legal@hireshelby.com. To report a security vulnerability, email security@hireshelby.com (see [Security](./security.md)).
