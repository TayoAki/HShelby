# Cookie notice

This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used on hireshelby.com and in the Hire Shelby dashboard at app.hireshelby.com, operated by HireShelby.

**In short: we use one cookie, only to keep you signed in to the dashboard. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't need to ask for your consent and there's no cookie banner.**

## The cookie we use

| Cookie | Where | What it does | Type | How long |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Session cookie set by our authentication system | app.hireshelby.com (the dashboard) | Keeps you signed in and ties your requests to your session, so only you can see your workspace | Strictly necessary, first-party. Not readable by scripts (HttpOnly), sent only over HTTPS (Secure), and not sent with cross-site requests (SameSite=Lax) | Up to 7 days, renewed while you use the dashboard. Deleted when you sign out |

The cookie holds a random session token, not your name or email address. Because it's strictly necessary to provide the dashboard you asked to use, the law doesn't require consent for it. If you block it, you won't be able to sign in.

## What we don't use

- No cookies at all on hireshelby.com.
- No analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies, pixels or scripts, on the website or in the dashboard.
- No local storage or similar browser storage in the dashboard.
- Our fonts are served from our own servers, not from a font service.

## Third parties you may see

- **Stripe.** When you subscribe or manage billing, you're taken to pages hosted by Stripe (Stripe Checkout and the billing portal). Stripe sets its own cookies there, for example for fraud prevention, under [Stripe's cookie policy](https://stripe.com/legal/cookies-policy).
- **Apple.** The dashboard shows your apps' icons, which your browser loads directly from Apple's servers. Apple therefore receives your IP address and browser details when an icon loads. We don't set or read cookies for Apple.

## Inside apps

Our SDK, in the apps that use Hire Shelby's chat, doesn't use cookies or advertising identifiers. It keeps a random device credential in the iOS Keychain, and the chat's state in the app's own storage, so the app can reconnect to its conversation; this is necessary for the chat to work. App developers describe this in their own privacy policies (see the [End-user privacy template](./end-user-privacy-template.md)).

## Changes and contact

If we add a cookie, we'll update this notice before we do, and if it isn't strictly necessary we'll ask for your consent first. Questions: privacy@hireshelby.com. More about how we handle personal data: [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).
